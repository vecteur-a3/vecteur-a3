## 2. Technical Specifications vs. Legal Compliance (GDPR, ePrivacy, PECR & CCPA)

Whether a specific web analytics deployment satisfies the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, the **ePrivacy Directive (Article 5(3))**, the **UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR)**, or US state privacy laws depends on your organization's specific data flows, legal basis, sub-processor agreements, and jurisdiction.

* **What We Report:** We strictly report what each software vendor **documents in its official technical documentation** as of our stated verification date — such as whether a script sets browser cookies by default, how visitor IP addresses are processed or masked, where collection and storage servers are located, and which Content Security Policy (CSP) directives are required.
* **What We Never Claim:** We never assert that any software tool *"is GDPR compliant,"* *"guarantees compliance,"* *"eliminates the need for a cookie banner,"* or is automatically *"legal"* in any jurisdiction.
* **Mandatory Consultation with Legal Counsel:** Always review each vendor's official Data Processing Agreement (DPA), sub-processor list, and current pricing/tax terms directly on the vendor's portal, and consult a qualified data privacy attorney or your appointed **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** before modifying your production telemetry stack.
